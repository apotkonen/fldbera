This booklet is central DataBase (DB) time diginatives User eXperience (UX)
case collection, from processes and law making originally designed for the
peoples having capability to remember 150 other community member peoples, have
calandar, pen, and some paper for notes and some community forms, but may not
have own clock, newspaper, network connection nor active devices on internet.
\\

This case collection lifts some widely used basic processes to the table, to
be seen and counted the price of existing setup, and then comparing it to
technososioeconomically-optimized diginatives version of it, to point out how
ridigilously we have costly overhead in some basic processes, we run widely
in our society.
\\

It might be that current law, designed for the small communities, demanded
this kind of operation, where all memory was on members head and on some
papers. We are living the era of fast digitalization, where all citizens
after forthcoming, next 25 years, are basically somekind of diginatives,
born after first digital calculators about 1975, being now 50's and 2050
about 75.
