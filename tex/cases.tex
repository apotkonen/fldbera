\section{Prescription recipe renew}
\label{prescription_renew}\index{prescription renew}

Many especially older peoples have medicine\allowbreak\cite{Laakeinfo}
prescription for long term health issue\allowbreak\cite{Kaypahoito}.
These prescriptions and medicine retrival data is stored to
Kanta-database\allowbreak\cite{BASE}. It's same database where epicrisis
and laboratory\allowbreak\cite{Labs} results are stored. It's somehow
amazing to see that there is no automatic prescription recipe reneval for
used medicines not requiring follow up, and ontime automatic laboratory
referral sending from medicines, requiring up to date followup, for
prescription reneval. Which then automatically reneved after resuls are
arrived to Kanta-database. Usually wellbeing services county is avare
from they own customers, and be able request nurse to take laboratory
samples from customer.

As we can see, well done service process digitalization is mostly invisible.
It's not requiring to use strong authentication through internet nor request
through occupational health services.

Now current situation is that elederly people who relatives is used to pick
medicines from pharmacy, and parmacy informs it be the last set in
prescription, can ask prescription reneval from pharmacy if community doctor
has written the prescription, but it's not accepted this way, elderly people
has to come personally in place to ask the prescription reneval.

Peoples having prescriptions from occupational health services has to ask
reneval from occupational services or by electronically requesting reneval
from Kanta-database services. It's not either automatic nor renovable trough
pharacy, even paharmacy hast prescription list open from Kanta-base. Needed
laboratory referreals are not automated nor informed either in this pharmacy
session.

This poorly optimized service digitalization is expected to cost as much of
customer value, from this semi-optimized situation, that it's seen as
business opportunity for commercial service\allowbreak\cite{Yle202609211442}.
Commercial service is expected to generate enough revenue from easing peoples
lives, to make it profitable business from these issues and possibbilities to
add prescription reneval, dosage services; vaccination, other medicine
injection, and perhaps laboratory sample take and delivery services to
pharmacy locations.

Commercial on pharmacy premises service could be handy, but it does not make
the basic process any better, it just make it more executable at customers
expense, still leaving process inbuild inefficensies in place where those
could be removed.
