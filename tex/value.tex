\chapter{Peoples resources valuation}
\label{valuation_people_resources}\index{valuation people resources}
When costs are evaluated from certain government organization perspective then
total cost is easily narrow mindedly defined, not takaing into account the tax
paying customer own resources; money, time, space, facilities, technology and
related peoples, like relatives time and resource usage. It does not either
count risks, ecological and psycological stress generated due risks in these
processes.

\section{Security risks valuation}
\label{valuation_security}\index{valuation security}
Each strong electronic identification means reliable verification of the
identity online. This verification process inludes psycological and
technological risks to be manipulated, misused then allowing abuse,
exploitation valueble to entire value of property assets person has and even
more, based to other persons name taken debt and purchases adding this debt. 
It depends a bit from laws each country has, but any way, even risk is low,
values what could be lost are big for the person under possible attac.

So when counting a risk and stress, it is much more expensive to demand
person book an appointment from system you has to use strong electronic
identification\allowbreak\cite{Korpela2025} than send a link to email
activating this appointment booking for person without strong identification
into system.

\section{Physical presense valuation}
\label{valuation_presense}\index{valuation presense}

When person is directly on inderectly required to be physically in place
there is transportation and timewise related costs. Depending from the person
situation transportation costs start from 0.1\euro/km by walking or by
bicycle to 2\euro/km level when needing special car and person to help.
All needed participants time has to be counted as worktime, because they
do customers mandatet or indirectly forced task to get things done right.
